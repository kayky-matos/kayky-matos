# About Me:
I architect cloud, AI, and security governance for organizations operating where technology, regulation, security, and business risk collide. As an AI Security and Governance Architect, I work across Generative AI, autonomous and multi-agent systems, cloud architectures, and AI supply chains, designing the governance boundaries that allow organizations to scale AI without scaling legal, financial, security, and operational exposure with it. 

My work focuses on translating complex AI risk into architectures that can actually be governed, defining risk boundaries, control models, architectural guidelines, acceptance criteria, and accountability structures that connect engineering decisions to regulatory obligations and business consequences.
